# SEO/GEO evidence framework

For each material finding record:

- URL or page cluster
- Observation
- Source
- Data period / retrieval date
- Market / device when relevant
- Fact vs inference vs hypothesis
- Priority
- Confidence
- Expected business effect
- Smallest proposed change
- Risk
- Validation / measurement method

## Useful decision questions

### Technical

- Does this affect crawl/index eligibility or intended canonical identity?
- Is the issue reproduced in current production or only source/tests?
- Is the signal a deterministic defect or a third-party warning?

### Commercial content

- What buyer question is unanswered or weakly answered?
- Does GSC show relevant impressions/queries for this page?
- Is another PicchuSpot page already satisfying that intent?
- Are competitors ranking because of content relevance, authority, product/offer differences, or something else?
- What is the smallest page change that tests the hypothesis?

### Cannibalization

Require evidence from queries/landing pages and intent. Do not merge pages because they share words.

### CTR

Use impressions, position, query mix, device, and market context. Do not assume a title rewrite is the cause of a later change without comparable evidence.

### AI search

Record engine/interface, prompt, country/language, date, search mode, answer/source URLs, PicchuSpot mention/citation, and factual errors. Repeat a fixed panel before interpreting movement.
